There was a space missing

Not a singles app.
Not full-on ENM.
Something in between.

We built Tandemly after looking for a place that felt right for regular, largely monogamous couples who were curious about a more monogamish edge. The alternatives felt too intense.

In conversations with other couples, we kept hearing a gentler ambition: meet another couple, build a real friendship, share good experiences and leave space for mutual attraction or chemistry without making it the whole point.

✦ That anecdotal insight shaped a calmer kind of app.
