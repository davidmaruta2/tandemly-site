How it works

Four steps from "hi" to an actual plan.

You and your partner build one profile together, then meet other couples who are looking for the same thing you are.

01

One shared profile

You and your partner each write your own part. No mixed signals, no guessing who's really talking - just one honest picture of you both.

02

Meet couples you click with

Browse couples nearby, wave at the ones you're curious about, and see who's waved back. Mutual? A chat opens automatically.

03

Plan a real meet

Pick an activity, a time and a place, send it as a proposal - your match confirms it in one tap.

04

Honest reviews

After you've actually met, both couples can leave a private review. It only goes live once both sides have shared theirs.

Built for trust

Yours to control, start to finish.

Report or block, one tap

If a match isn't a good fit, ending it and reporting it takes a moment - no explanation owed.

Your location stays yours

Other couples only ever see an approximate area and distance - never your exact location.

Real couples, real photos

Every profile photo is of the two of you together - never a stranger's, never AI-generated.
