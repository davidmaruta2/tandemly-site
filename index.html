How it works

One profile, built by both of you.

You and your partner write your Tandem profile together, then meet other couples who are looking for the same thing you are.

01

One shared profile

You and your partner each write your own part of a single profile. No mixed signals, no guessing who you're really talking to - just one honest picture of you both.

02

Meet couples you click with

Browse couples nearby, wave at the ones you're curious about, and see who's waved back. When it's mutual, a chat opens automatically.

03

Plan a real meet

Once you're chatting, pick an activity, a time and a place, and send it as a proposal your match can confirm in one tap.

04

Honest reviews after you meet

Once a planned meet has happened, both couples can leave a private review. Reviews only go live once both sides have shared theirs, so nobody holds back.
